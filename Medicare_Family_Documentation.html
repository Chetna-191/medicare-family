🩺 MediCare Family

Medicine Reminder & Family Schedule Manager — System Guide & User Manual

1. Executive Summary & Overview

MediCare Family is a full-stack web application designed to solve medication mismanagement in multi-generational households. It centralizes prescription schedules, prevents conflicting and duplicate doses, enables instant 1-click dose tracking, and provides 7-day adherence analytics.

Key Objectives Solved:
  • Multi-Member Routines: Individual profiles for elderly grandparents, parents, and children under one family login.
  • Zero Duplicates: Strict backend logic prevents overlapping prescription names and duplicate daily timings.
  • Interactive Accountability: 1-click dose logging (Taken / Skip / Undo) with real-time adherence scoring and streak tracking.

3. Step-by-Step Page & Feature Operation Guide

A. Authentication & Demo Mode (/login)

  • 1-Click Instant Demo Login: Click "Sign In as Demo Family (1-Click)" to instantly explore pre-configured data for The Miller Family (Arthur, Sarah, Leo) with 8 active medications and 7-day historical logs.
  • Standard Login: Enter email (demo@medicare.family) and password (demo1234) and click "Sign In to Dashboard".
  • Register New Family: Click "Don't have an account yet? Create one", input family name, email, and password to initialize a fresh, isolated workspace.

B. Dashboard & Daily Schedule (/)

  • Daily Progress Overview: Real-time progress bar showing today's taken vs. pending doses and adherence percentage.
  • Date Switcher: Navigate rapidly using "Yesterday", "Today", "Tomorrow", or select any custom date via the calendar picker.
  • Multi-Filter: Filter visible doses by specific family member or by time of day (Morning: 00:00-11:59, Afternoon: 12:00-16:59, Evening: 17:00-23:59).
  • 1-Click Dose Actions:
    • "Mark Taken": Marks dose completed, records exact timestamp, and triggers celebration confetti.
    • "Skip": Logs dose as intentionally skipped.
    • "Undo": Reverts accidental clicks back to pending status.

C. Family Members Management (/members)

  • Add Member: Click "+ Add Member", enter name, relation (Grandfather, Mother, Child, etc.), age, and choose a custom avatar theme.
  • Individual Member Profile (/members/:id): View personalized adherence rates, all active prescriptions, and quickly add medicines specifically for that member.
  • Edit / Delete: Update member details or remove members with automatic cascade cleanup of their medications and logs.

D. Medicine & Prescription Scheduling (/medicines)

  • Add Prescription: Click "+ Add Medicine", choose member, enter medication name, dosage (e.g., 500mg, 1 tablet), instructions, and start/end dates.
  • Multi-Timing Presets: Add one or multiple daily dosing times using fast presets (Morning 08:00, Noon 13:00, Night 20:30) or custom 24-hr entries.
  • Backend Duplicate Validation: Automatically blocks identical medicine names for the same member during overlapping date intervals.

E. Adherence Analytics (/adherence)

  • 7-Day & 14-Day View: Toggle historical windows to monitor consistency trends.
  • Streak Counter: Shows consecutive days of 100% medication compliance (e.g. 🔥 6-Day Perfect Streak).
  • Interactive Charts: Visual comparison of daily scheduled doses vs. taken doses across all members.
  • Weekly Status Grid: Per-member matrix breakdown indicating daily completed vs. missed doses.
